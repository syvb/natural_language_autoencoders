**Assistant:** Here is the step-by-step breakdown:

1.  **Calculate the total cost:**
    Multiply the number of notebooks by the price per notebook.

2.  **Calculate the change:**
    Subtract the total cost from the amount paid.
    $$\$50.00 - \$22.75 = \$27.25$$
