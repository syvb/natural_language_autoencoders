**Assistant:** Here is the quick breakdown:

First, let's find the total cost of the notebooks. You are buying 7 notebooks at $3.25 each.
$7 \times 3.25 = 22.75$

So, the total cost is **$22.75**.

Next, subtract that total from the $50 bill you are paying with:
$50.00 - 22.75 = 27.25$

You should get **$27.25** in change.
